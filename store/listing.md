# NomadFS — Google Play listing

## App name (30)

NomadFS

## Short description (80)

Sync, back up and stream files between your own devices. No account, no cloud.

## Full description (4000)

NomadFS joins your phone, Chromebook, computers and home server into one private filesystem. Your files travel directly between your own devices, encrypted, with no account and no cloud service in the middle.

THE SWARM FOLDER
One folder every one of your devices shares. Add a file on your phone and it's on your laptop; rename or delete it anywhere. Files download only when you open them, so a small phone can see a large library.

BACK UP YOUR PHOTOS
Mark your Camera folder for backup and every device you choose to keep copies (a home server, a computer) keeps one. Photos keep their original dates, and once they're backed up you can free space on the phone.

BROWSE YOUR OTHER DEVICES
Open the folders your other devices share as if they were here. Preview photos, PDFs and text, and play videos and music while they stream: playback starts at once and seeks anywhere, even for large files.

DOWNLOADS YOU CAN FIND
Download a file and follow its progress. The Downloads view shows where each one was saved, plays it, opens it in another app or shows its folder.

WORKS ON ANDROID PHONES, TABLETS AND CHROMEBOOKS
A phone layout with a bottom bar, and a desktop layout with a sidebar and right-click menus on tablets and Chromebooks. NomadFS also runs on Linux desktops and servers, with the same swarm.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.
• Your devices pair with a one-time invite code or QR code; the swarm key never travels in the clear.
• Connections are encrypted and authenticated between your devices.
• Your devices relay for each other when they can't reach each other directly.

PERMISSIONS
• All files access: to share and back up the folders you choose, and to save downloads where other apps can open them.
• Camera: only to scan an invite QR code.
• Notifications: to keep NomadFS running in the background when you turn that on.

NomadFS is open source: https://github.com/highercomve/nomadfs
